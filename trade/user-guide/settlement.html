NadaTrade Documentation

Settlement

NadaTrade implements a full post-trade lifecycle: position tracking, realized P&L computation, settlement obligations, and mark-to-market valuation. This guide covers the mechanics of how your positions, cash, and settlement obligations are managed after each trade.

1. Position Tracking

Each participant holds one Position per instrument. A position is identified by (participant_id, instrument_id) and stored in the Ledger. A position has no explicit side field—the sign of qty determines the direction:

  • Positive qty → long position
  • Negative qty → short position
FieldTypeDescription
qtyDecimalCurrent position size. Positive = long, negative = short.
avg_costDecimalVolume-weighted average entry price.
realized_pnlDecimalCumulative realized profit/loss from closed portions of the position.
unrealized_pnlDecimalCurrent mark-to-market P&L (see §9).

Positions are lazily initialized to qty=0, avg_cost=0, realized_pnl=0, unrealized_pnl=0 on the first trade for that instrument.

2. apply_fill — Position Update Logic

Every TradePrinted event triggers an apply_fill call on both the buyer’s and the seller’s positions. A fill carries:

  • fill_qty: positive for buys, negative for sells (the sign matters)
  • fill_price: the execution price

The function handles three cases:

2a. Extend

When the fill is on the same side as the existing position (both qty values have the same sign), the position is extended:

new_qty = old_qty + fill_qty
avg_cost = (|old_qty| × old_avg + |fill_qty| × fill_price) / (|old_qty| + |fill_qty|)

Example. Holding +500 PETR4 at avg 38.50. Buy another +300 at 39.00:

avg_cost = (500 × 38.50 + 300 × 39.00) / (500 + 300) = 38.6875
new_qty  = +800
realized_pnl = 0 (no portion closed)

2b. Reduce

When the fill is on the opposite side (signs differ), a portion of the position is closed. Realized P&L is calculated on the closed portion only:

reduced = min(|old_qty|, |fill_qty|)
realized = reduced × (fill_price − avg_cost) × position_sign

The position sign (old_sign) is +1 for longs, −1 for shorts. This yields:

  • Closing longs (sell against long): reduced × (fill_price − avg_cost) — profit when fill_price > avg_cost
  • Closing shorts (buy back against short): reduced × (avg_cost − fill_price) — profit when fill_price < avg_cost

The remaining qty keeps the original avg_cost.

Example. Holding +500 PETR4 at 38.50. Sell 300 at 39.00:

reduced = min(500, 300) = 300
realized = 300 × (39.00 − 38.50) × (+1) = 150.00
new_qty  = +200
avg_cost = 38.50 (unchanged)

Example. Short −500 PETR4 at 40.00. Buy back 300 at 39.00:

reduced = min(500, 300) = 300
realized = 300 × (39.00 − 40.00) × (−1) = 300.00
new_qty  = −200
avg_cost = 40.00 (unchanged)

2c. Flip

When the fill qty exceeds the position in the opposite direction, the position flips:

  1. Close the entire original position → realize full P&L on the original side
  2. Open a new opposite-side position at the fill price
realized = |old_qty| × (fill_price − old_avg) × old_sign
avg_cost = fill_price

Example. Holding +500 PETR4 at 38.50. Sell 800 at 39.00:

realized = 500 × (39.00 − 38.50) × (+1) = 250.00
avg_cost = 39.00
new_qty  = −300 (now short)

Example. Short −500 PETR4 at 40.00. Buy 700 at 39.00:

realized = 500 × (39.00 − 40.00) × (−1) = 500.00
avg_cost = 39.00
new_qty  = +200 (now long)

3. The Ledger

The Ledger struct is the per-participant balance sheet:

FieldTypeDescription
participantParticipantIdOwner of this ledger.
cashHashMap<Currency, Decimal>Cash balances per currency.
positionsHashMap<InstrumentId, Position>One position per instrument traded.
pending_obligationsVec<SettlementObligation>Unsettled T+N obligations (see §6–8).
realized_pnlDecimalRunning total of all realized P&L across positions.

Cash tracking

On each trade, cash is adjusted immediately:

  • Buyer: cash − (qty × price) in the instrument’s currency
  • Seller: cash + (qty × price) in the instrument’s currency

Cash is tracked per currency (e.g., "BRL", "USD"). The currency comes from the instrument’s InstrumentConfig.settlement.currency field.

Ledger lifecycle

  • A ledger is created lazily on the first trade for a participant.
  • Positions are created lazily per instrument.
  • Settlement can later adjust cash (see §8).

4. Realized P&L

Realized P&L is credited to the realized_pnl field on the Ledger each time apply_fill closes any qty. The formula depends on direction:

DirectionActionFormula
LongSell (close long)qty × (sell_price − avg_cost)
ShortBuy (close short)qty × (avg_cost − buy_price)

Example — Long P&L. Bought 500 at 38.50. Sold 200 at 39.00:

P&L = 200 × (39.00 − 38.50) = +100.00

Example — Short P&L. Sold short 300 at 42.00. Bought back 150 at 40.00:

P&L = 150 × (42.00 − 40.00) = +300.00

Realized P&L is cumulative and never resets. It is not the same as mark-to-market (unrealized) P&L. Only closed portions generate realized P&L.

5. Settlement Cycle (T+N)

Each instrument defines a settlement cycle in its configuration under settlement.cycle:

CycleDescriptionSettlement date
T0Same-day settlementTrade date
T1Next business dayTrade date + 1 day
T2Two business days (default)Trade date + 2 days
T3Three business daysTrade date + 3 days
TN(int)Custom N daysTrade date + N days

Unknown cycle values default to T2.

The settlement cycle is configured per instrument:

[[instruments]]
name = "PETR4"
# ...
[instruments.settlement]
cycle = "T2"
currency = "BRL"

6. Settlement Date Calculation

The settlement date is calculated as trade_date + N calendar days, where N is determined by the instrument’s settlement cycle (§5).

For session-based instruments with trading_days, the system uses InstrumentConfig.trading_days (e.g., ["Mon", "Tue", "Wed", "Thu", "Fri"]) to skip weekends and holidays when needed by higher-level scheduling logic. The current implementation computes the settlement date as a simple calendar offset from the trade’s sim_time.

This means a trade on Friday with T+2 would settle on Sunday (calendar date). The sweep function then checks if the current simulation date equals or exceeds this date.

7. Settlement Obligations

When create_obligations() is called for each TradePrinted event, it creates one SettlementObligation per side of the trade:

FieldDescription
trade_idThe UUID of the trade.
participantWhich participant owes or is owed.
instrumentThe instrument traded.
qtyQuantity traded.
priceExecution price.
sideSide::Buy or Side::Sell.
cashNotional amount (qty × price).
currencySettlement currency (from instrument config).
settle_dateThe date when this obligation becomes due.
settledfalse initially; set to true after sweep.

Buyer’s obligation: cash is positive (the amount owed to the counterparty). On settlement, this amount is debited from the buyer’s cash balance.

Seller’s obligation: cash is also positive (the amount the seller receives). On settlement, this amount is credited to the seller’s cash balance.

Obligations are appended to the participant’s pending_obligations list in their ledger.

Synthetic trades

Trades with synthetic = true (e.g., from simulation or mark-to-market adjustments) are skipped in settlement — they generate no obligations. Only real trades originating from the matching engine create obligations.

8. Settlement Sweep

sweep_settlements() runs periodically. In the current implementation, it is triggered at end-of-day (when the session transitions to Closed).

Sweep logic

For each participant’s pending obligations:

  1. If settle_date ≤ today and settled == false:
    • Buyer side: debit cash from the participant’s cash balance.
    • Seller side: credit cash to the participant’s cash balance.
    • Mark the obligation as settled = true.
    • Emit an ObligationSettled event.
  2. If settle_date > today, leave the obligation in the pending list.

Future-dated obligations are never settled early. Once settled, obligations are retained in the pending list with settled = true (they are not removed).

Settlement events

Each settled obligation emits an ExchangeEvent::ObligationSettled:

{
  "participant": "BOT001",
  "trade_id": "f47ac10b-...",
  "instrument": "PETR4",
  "qty": "100.0",
  "cash": "3850.00",
  "currency": "BRL",
  "sim_time": ...
}

Worked example

  • BOT001 buys 100 PETR4 @ 38.50 from BOT002 on Monday.
  • Settlement cycle: T2, so settle_date = Wednesday.
  • On Monday and Tuesday the sweep runs but skips this obligation (settle_date is in the future).
  • On Wednesday, the sweep fires:
    • BOT001 cash: −3,850.00 BRL
    • BOT002 cash: +3,850.00 BRL
    • Obligation marked settled.

Mixed obligations

A participant can have obligations with different settlement dates. The sweep processes only those that have matured:

pending_obligations = [
  { settle_date: 2026-06-09, settled: true  },  // already swept earlier
  { settle_date: 2026-06-12, settled: false },  // sweeps today
  { settle_date: 2026-06-15, settled: false },  // stays pending
]

9. Mark-to-Market (Unrealized P&L)

mark_to_market() computes the unrealized P&L on open positions based on the most recent trade price:

unrealized_pnl = current_qty × (last_price − avg_cost)

This is called on every TradePrinted event, using the trade price as the mark. For example:

  • Position: long 1,000 PETR4 @ avg 38.50

  • Last trade: 40.00

  • Unrealized P&L = 1000 × (40.00 − 38.50) = +1,500.00 (BRL, since PETR4 is BRL-denominated)

  • Position: short 500 PETR4 @ avg 42.00

  • Last trade: 40.00

  • Unrealized P&L = −500 × (40.00 − 42.00) = +1,000.00

Key properties

  • Informational only. Mark-to-market does not affect cash balances, realized P&L, or settlement.
  • Per-instrument. Only positions in the instrument whose trade just occurred are re-marked. Other instruments retain their last mark.
  • Flat positions ignored. If qty == 0, unrealized P&L is left unchanged.
  • Used by the TUI Dashboard (Ledger tab), risk monitoring, and external reporting.

10. Synthetic Trades

A TradePrinted event carries a synthetic boolean field:

  • synthetic: false — real trade from the matching engine. Positions are updated, obligations are created, settlement applies.
  • synthetic: true — generated trade (e.g., from simulation scenarios, replay data, or system adjustments). Position tracking still applies, but no settlement obligations are created. Realized P&L is still computed for the ledger.

This allows synthetic data feeds to exercise position tracking without polluting the settlement pipeline.

11. Continuous Instruments (24×7)

Instruments with session.type = "continuous_24x7" never enter a Closed session state, so the EOD sweep trigger does not fire. For these instruments:

  • Settlement obligations are still created with appropriate settle_date values (T+0, T+2, etc.).
  • The sweep function processes obligations whenever it is called. Integration code should invoke sweep_settlements() on a periodic timer (e.g., every simulated midnight) for continuous instruments.
  • No trading-day gating applies — every calendar day is a valid settlement date.

The trading_days configuration (e.g., ["Mon", "Tue", "Wed", "Thu", "Fri"]) does not constrain settlement for continuous instruments.

12. Currency

Every instrument declares a settlement currency:

[instruments.settlement]
cycle = "T2"
currency = "BRL"

All cash tracking and settlement amounts use this currency. A single participant’s ledger can hold balances in multiple currencies (e.g., BRL from PETR4 trades, USD from ADR trades). Each currency bucket is tracked independently in ledger.cash: HashMap<Currency, Decimal>.

Currency flow summary

StepWhere the currency field is used
Trade executionInstrumentConfig.settlement.currency
Cash debit/creditsettle_trade() adjusts ledger.cash[currency]
Obligation recordSettlementObligation.currency
Settlement sweepSweep moves cash using the obligation’s currency field
MtM displayP&L is expressed in the instrument’s currency

Full Lifecycle Example

Consider BOT001 trading PETR4 (currency = BRL, T+2 settlement):

DayEventPositionCash (BRL)Pending Obligations
Mon 09:30Buy 1,000 @ 38.50+1,000 @ 38.50−38,5001 (settles Wed)
Mon 10:00Sell 300 @ 39.00+700 @ 38.50−26,8001 (qty 1,000)
Mon 10:01Realized P&L: 300 × (39.00 − 38.50) = +150.00
Wed 00:01Sweep runs (settle_date ≤ today)+700 @ 38.50−26,8001 (settled)

After settlement, BOT001’s cash reflects the full consideration for the trade. The realized P&L of 150.00 is embedded in the position tracking but does not change the cash flow (the seller receives the full 38,500 regardless of BOT001’s average cost).

The unrealized P&L on the remaining 700 shares will fluctuate with each subsequent trade price update via mark_to_market, visible in the TUI Dashboard Ledger tab.
